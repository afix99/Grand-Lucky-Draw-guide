🔢 How to Qualify — Quick Reference

Any purchase → 🎲 1 Mini Lucky Draw spin (on the spot!)
RM100 nett → 💸 RM20 rebate + 🎰 x1 Grand Entry
RM180 nett → 🎰 x2 Grand Entries + 🛍️ PWP Nekozilla at RM19
RM250 nett → 🎁 FREE Nekozilla Set (RM79 value!) + 🎰 x2 Grand Entries

🎁 What is GWP vs PWP?

  • GWP (Gift With Purchase) = FREE — e.g., Nekozilla Set free when you spend RM250
  • PWP (Purchase With Purchase) = Discounted add-on — e.g., Nekozilla Set at only RM19 when you spend RM180

💡 At RM180, the customer can buy the set for RM19. At RM250, they get it free.

🎲 Mini Lucky Draw (MLD) — Quick Facts

  • ✅ ANY purchase qualifies — no minimum spend
  • ✅ 1 draw per transaction
  • ✅ Prizes redeemed on the spot, immediately
  • ⚠️ Vouchers valid on normal price items only (unless the prize says "FREE")
⚠️ Entry Cap: Maximum 2 Grand Lucky Draw entries per transaction — even if total spend exceeds RM360.

🔄 Step-by-Step Selling Flow

1

First Purchase — Push to the Right Tier

Before checkout, you must already know the customer's basket total and your upsell target.

  • Push to RM100 → RM20 rebate + x1 entry + Mini Draw spin
  • Best push to RM180 → x2 entries + Mini Draw + PWP Nekozilla RM19
  • Max push to RM250 → FREE Nekozilla Set + x2 entries + Mini Draw

💡 Tip: Know the gap. "You're at RM140 — just RM40 more and you get double entries AND a discounted Nekozilla set at RM19!"

2

Mini Lucky Draw — Instant Excitement 🎉

"Tahniah! You get a FREE Mini Lucky Draw right now!" — Customer draws their prize on the spot.

Hand the draw slip right after payment. Don't let them leave without doing it!

3

⭐ Upsell Using the MLD Win (MOST IMPORTANT STEP)

Immediately after they draw, use the prize to push a second purchase.

  • If 1st purchase below RM180 nett:
    "You can use your voucher now! Top up a bit to RM180 nett — we combine the receipts, so you get double Grand Lucky Draw entries too 🔥"
  • If 1st purchase already RM180+ nett:
    "Wah, good deal! Grab the jersey you like at half price now, only RM22. Better get 2 pieces to fully use your voucher — super worth!"

✅ You can combine today's receipts (original + Mini Draw receipt) when registering the Grand Lucky Draw.

4

Register for Grand Lucky Draw (LAST STEP)

"Please scan this QR code to register your Lucky Draw entry. Make sure your phone number is correct — we'll call you if you win! 👍"

  • Collect: Name, Phone, MyKad No., Purchase Date, State, Total Purchase Amount
  • Combine same-day receipts before filling in the total
  • Staff must guide and double-check all details
