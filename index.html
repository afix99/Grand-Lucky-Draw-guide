🎁 What is GWP vs PWP?

  • GWP = Gift With Purchase = FREE (e.g., Nekozilla at RM250)
  • PWP = Purchase With Purchase = Discounted add-on (e.g., Nekozilla at RM19 with RM180 spend)

🎲 Mini Lucky Draw (MLD)

  • Any purchase — no minimum spend
  • 1 draw per transaction
  • Prizes redeemed on the spot
  • Vouchers valid on normal price items only (unless stated FREE)

Tier 2 — Best Push
RM180 Nett price
  • ✅ 2 Grand Lucky Draw Entries

RM250 Nett price
